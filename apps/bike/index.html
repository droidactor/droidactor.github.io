Dotori Korea Bike

com.droidactor.bike

Ride Korea's 13 national cross-country bike paths without an account, sign-in or subscription. Follow a path, see the distance to the certification centers and facilities ahead, read a large-font cycle computer and keep multi-day section progress on your phone.

A riding companion for Korea's national cross-country paths

Made to be used alongside the official certification app while you ride: the path, what is ahead of you, a readable cycle computer, screen-off recording and your own progress, all kept on your device. Not yet published on Google Play.

  • 13 national paths in 14 riding courses: Ara, Hangang (South and North banks as separate courses), Namhangang, Saejae, Nakdonggang, Geumgang, Yeongsangang, Bukhangang, Seomjingang, Ocheon, East Coast (Gangwon and Gyeongbuk) and Jeju Fantasy.
  • Certification centers and facilities: 88 certification centers with staffed or unstaffed status and opening hours, plus restrooms, water fountains and air pumps. Facilities that cannot be placed on the path with confidence, such as those across a river or on a side branch, are listed for reference only instead of being shown as the next stop on the path.
  • Path following and off-route alerts: while recording, follow a national path, a saved GPX route or a previous ride. You get an alert when you stay away from the selected path, and the distance along the path to the next center, restroom, water fountain or air pump. When you are off the path, the distance is clearly labelled as straight-line. Switch the path direction at any time.
  • Certification center approach alert: near a center, a notification offers a button that opens the official Bicycle Happy Sharing app of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety (MOIS), or its Google Play page when it is not installed.
  • Large-font cycle computer: current speed, moving average, max speed, distance, moving time, ascent, altitude and time, with keep-screen-on, a landscape mount layout and a full-screen mode without the banner.
  • Screen-off recording: a ride you start keeps recording with a notification while the screen is off, without background location permission. Pauses and GPS gaps are shown as unlinked segments rather than filled in.
  • Personal section progress: rides from several days add up per center-to-center section. A section passed with large GPS gaps is marked "Confirm needed" instead of being completed automatically, and you can mark any section completed or not ridden yourself.
  • Records and GPX: review each ride on a map with speed and altitude charts and a memo, and import or export GPX through Android's file picker.
  • Current Location · 119: shows your coordinates, Korea National Point Number, accuracy and measurement time. Copy them, or open a 119 text draft in your messaging app, add what happened and send it yourself.

Use it with the official certification app

Official cyber certification for the national cross-country paths is done in the MOIS Bicycle Happy Sharing app, a separate official app. Dotori Korea Bike is an independent riding companion built on Korean public data. It does not represent and is not affiliated with MOIS, K-water or any other government agency. It only opens that app with a normal launch, does not replace or automate certification, and keeps its personal section progress separate from official certification records.

Every feature free

All features are free with no account, subscription or in-app purchase. A banner ad is shown at the top of the screen, except in the full-screen cycle computer, and advertising privacy choices are offered where required.

Before you ride and known limitations

  • Covers the 13 national cross-country paths (14 courses) only. Other bike roads, scenic and island bike paths, and public bike rental stations are not included.
  • This is not turn-by-turn navigation. It shows the selected path, off-route alerts and distances to what is ahead.
  • Path and facility data come from public datasets with fixed reference dates (see Data sources). Construction, detours, closures, and whether a center or facility is open or working may have changed. Always follow on-site signs, detours and traffic laws.
  • Path data is built into the app, but the Google Maps base map needs a network connection.
  • GPS accuracy depends on your phone and surroundings. Sections with large GPS gaps need your confirmation.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later.

Data sources

  • Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Korea national cycling paths DB (Public Data Portal 3038533, reference date 2024-02-29): paths, certification centers and facilities.
  • K-water, cross-country cycling certification centers (Public Data Portal 15006196, reference date 2026-09-14): staffed status and opening hours of the centers.

Both datasets are published without usage restrictions. Major changes announced after 2024-02-29 are listed on the app's Sources & Data Dates screen.

Privacy

Rides, memos, favorites, saved GPX routes, section progress and settings stay in app-private storage on your phone and are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. There is no account and no developer server for ride data. Precise location is used while you record a ride and while the Current Location · 119 screen is open; background location is not requested. Ride data is not sent to the developer, the advertising SDK, the official certification app or any government agency. Google Maps, Google AdMob/UMP, Play updates and signed static operational notices from droidactor.github.io use the network. Map requests may reveal the viewed area to Google, and under available privacy choices the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers.
