com.droidactor.vault

Encrypt photos, videos and documents on your phone. View files in the vault and make encrypted backups.

Files beyond photos

Add photos, videos, PDFs, text, documents and other files using system pickers or Share. Organize them into folders. View supported photos, videos, PDFs and text in the vault; use another app for other formats.

Check before deleting originals

The app decrypts an imported copy and checks its size and SHA-256 hash before offering original deletion. Deletion requires your confirmation and the source provider's permission. It is ordinary deletion, not secure erasure.

Unlock and recover

Use a password, a supported fingerprint or both. Turning the screen off locks the vault. Leaving the app also locks it; file selection, deletion confirmation and opening a file in another app allow a grace period of up to five minutes. Keep your recovery key outside the phone. Make an encrypted backup before uninstalling or changing phones. A password or recovery key alone cannot recreate deleted vault data. File export and encrypted backup do not require a subscription or payment.

Protection limits

The normal app blocks screenshots and recent-app previews. Exported files, external backups and copies made by other apps are outside the vault. Protection on a compromised or rooted phone is not guaranteed. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Includes a banner ad placement. English, Korean, light and dark themes are supported.

Privacy

Files, file names, folders and previews are encrypted in the app's private storage on this phone. Vault passwords, recovery keys and file contents are not sent to the developer or advertising SDK. Android handles fingerprint authentication; the app does not receive fingerprint images. There is no developer account or cloud sync. System pickers and Share provide access only to files you choose; broad storage, location, contacts, camera and microphone permissions are not requested. If you save a backup or export through a cloud document provider, that provider may upload it at your request. Files opened in another app may be copied by that app. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are excluded. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes the local vault; only an earlier backup can restore it. External backups and exported files must be deleted where you saved them. Google AdMob/UMP and Play updates may use the network. Under available privacy choices, the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Settings offers available advertising privacy options. Contact droidactor@gmail.com for privacy questions.
