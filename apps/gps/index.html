Dotori GPS Tracker

com.droidactor.gps

Save locations, record journeys and export GPX. Your records stay on your device.

Coming to Google Play

Features

  • Save a location with a note
  • Time, distance or combined recording intervals
  • Pause, resume, route statistics and route preview
  • Open external maps, share and export GPX
  • JSON backup/restore, English/Korean and themes

Requirements and limits

Android 8.0+. Location permission is required for measurement. A journey you start can continue with the screen off until you pause or finish it. Accuracy depends on device and surroundings. The route preview has no map background. Not an emergency or surveying service.

Privacy

Coordinates, routes and notes stay on the device. External maps and exports transfer only what you choose. Google Mobile Ads processes separate advertising data.
