Map and search

20 stations. Korean and English station names are available; Japanese and Chinese station names fall back to English. No journey planning, travel times, live arrivals or fares. Follow operator announcements for service information.

An original vector map using Korea National Railway/KRIC station names and coordinates, with station order from KRIC station numbering. This is an independent app; it does not use the operator map artwork.

Android 8.0+ · Google AdMob

Nearby station

With your permission, the app checks device location once when starting to select a nearby station. Coordinates are processed on your device and are not sent to a developer server or included in advertising requests. If permission or a location fix is unavailable, the map starts at Culture Center. There is no background location tracking.

Privacy

Search text, selected stations and lines, and label choices are processed locally. There is no account, journey-history server or separate analytics service. Android backup is disabled. Clear app storage to remove local app data.

The app uses a Google AdMob banner and UMP privacy choices. Google may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Available privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Clearing app data does not delete information already held by Google.
