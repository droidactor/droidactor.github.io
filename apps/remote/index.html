Dotori PC Video Remote

com.droidactor.bt_remote

Bluetooth remote for YouTube on your PC.

Pair your Android phone with your PC as a standard Bluetooth keyboard. Control YouTube in the PC web browser without installing a driver or companion program on the computer.

Coming soonPrivacy

Controls within reach

  • Play and pause, seek by 5 or 10 seconds, and adjust volume.
  • Toggle mute, fullscreen, cinema mode and captions.
  • Change playback speed, step through frames while paused, and move between playlist videos.
  • Reconnect to a previously verified PC, optionally connect on startup, and switch PCs from connection history.

Before pressing a button

Pair the phone and PC over Bluetooth, select the PC in the app and complete the connection. Open YouTube in the PC web browser and focus its video player. Use the built-in shortcut reference for the key sent by each control.

Requirements and limits

Requires Android 9.0 or newer and a phone that supports the Bluetooth HID Device profile. Supports YouTube in PC web browsers. Other video sites, desktop media players and TV apps are not supported. Keys can reach another window if it has focus. Phone power-saving settings may end the background connection.

Privacy

Controls travel directly to the selected PC over Bluetooth. There is no app account or developer backend. The app includes a Google AdMob banner and UMP privacy choices.
