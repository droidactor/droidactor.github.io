Dotori Tokyo Metro

Explore 13 Tokyo subway lines, search multilingual station names and plan offline diagram routes.

Offline map and diagram routes

View the 9 Tokyo Metro and 4 Toei subway lines. Search station names or numbers, select departure and arrival stations, and follow highlighted route sections. Routes prioritize fewer line changes, then fewer stops. A line change count is not the actual number of train changes.

Station names are available in Japanese, English, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. French and Spanish names cover Tokyo Metro stations; missing translations use English. The interface supports 12 languages.

No train-specific routes, travel times, distances, full departure timetables or live arrivals. JR and private railways are outside the route network. Follow signs and operator announcements. This is an independent app, not an official app of the operators or the Tokyo Metropolitan Government.

Fare information is available with its reference date and conditions. Directional first/last train summaries cover the four Toei lines; the nine Tokyo Metro lines have no first/last summaries.

Nearby stations and privacy

Location access is optional. Nearest-station lookup covers Tokyo Metro and Toei stations with bundled geographic coordinates. Your position is compared on the device, without saving it or adding it to advertising requests. When location services are unavailable, the default station is Shinjuku. Schematic map positions are not geographic coordinates.

There is no account or developer-operated server for searches or journeys. Language settings are stored locally. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled. Google AdMob separately processes advertising data, and UMP provides applicable privacy choices. Map viewing, searches and diagram routes work offline; advertising and Google Play app updates use a network connection.
