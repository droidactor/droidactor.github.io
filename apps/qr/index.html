com.droidactor.qr

Scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera, a photo or a shared image. See where a link goes before you open it.

Results right away

There is no full-screen ad or upgrade screen after each scan. The only ad is one banner at the top. Links, contacts and Wi-Fi networks never open or save on their own; you choose what to do on the result screen. Every feature is free, with no subscription or in-app purchase.

Check links before opening

The place a link goes to is shown in large type with the full address below it. Notes point out a short-link service, an address without encryption (http), an IP number, international characters, user information before an @ sign, or an app install file (.apk). The notes describe how the address looks; they do not judge whether a site is safe.

Only the code in the box

Codes outside the guide box are not read. If several codes are inside the box, you choose one. Photos from the gallery and images shared from other apps, such as chat apps and screenshots, are searched as a whole and are not saved.

Actions and history

Add Wi-Fi networks, contacts and events through system confirmation screens; call, text, email, open a location in a map app, search a product barcode on the web, copy and share. Scans are saved on this phone, up to 500, with search and delete. Supported codes: QR code, Aztec, Data Matrix, PDF417, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar and ITF. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Includes a banner ad. English, Korean, light and dark themes are supported.

Privacy

The camera view, photos and shared images are used only to read codes on this phone; they are not stored or sent anywhere. Scan history is kept only in the app's private storage on this phone and is excluded from automatic Android backup and device transfer. You can turn off saving history in Settings, and history is deleted from the history screen or when you uninstall the app. There is no developer account, server or cloud sync. Only the camera permission is requested; location, contacts, calendar, broad storage and microphone permissions are not requested. Saving a contact, event or Wi-Fi network, calling, texting, emailing, opening a map, searching the web and sharing hand the content to a system app or an app you choose, and that app's own policy applies afterwards. Google AdMob/UMP and Play updates may use the network. Under available privacy choices, the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Settings offers advertising privacy options. Contact droidactor@gmail.com for privacy questions.
