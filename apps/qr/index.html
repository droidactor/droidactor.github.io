Dotori QR & Barcode Scanner

com.droidactor.qr

Scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera, a photo or a shared image. See where a link goes before you open it. You can also create QR codes and barcodes to share.

Results right away

There is no full-screen ad or upgrade screen after each scan. The only ad is one banner at the top. Links, contacts and Wi-Fi networks never open or save on their own; you choose what to do on the result screen. Every feature is free, with no subscription or in-app purchase.

Check links before opening

The place a link goes to is shown in large type with the full address below it. Notes point out a short-link service, an address without encryption (http), an IP number, international characters, user information before an @ sign, or an app install file (.apk). The notes describe how the address looks; they do not judge whether a site is safe.

Only the code in the box

Codes outside the guide box are not read. If several codes are inside the box, you choose one. Photos from the gallery and images shared from other apps, such as chat apps and screenshots, are searched as a whole and are not saved.

Create QR codes and barcodes

Choose what a QR code holds (text, link, Wi-Fi, contact, phone number, text message or email), fill in the fields, and the code is drawn as you type. Barcodes come in the same 12 formats the app reads; for EAN and UPC the check digit is added for you. Share a created code as an image or save it to your gallery (Android 10 and later). On a scan result, Show as code puts the scanned code on screen so someone else can scan it. Codes you share or save are added to history marked Created.

Share current Wi-Fi

In Create → Wi-Fi, Share current Wi-Fi opens the system Wi-Fi QR sharing screen without typing a password. It uses the Android AccessibilityService API: only when you tap the button, the app reads the Wi-Fi screen in Settings and taps the connected network and QR sharing button for you, then stops. The first time, you accept an in-app explanation and turn the service on yourself in Accessibility settings; you can turn it off at any time. You complete fingerprint or PIN verification yourself. Without accessibility access you can still create a QR code by entering the Wi-Fi name and password. Some phones have different settings screens, so the automatic steps may not work.

Actions and history

Add Wi-Fi networks, contacts and events through system confirmation screens; call, text, email, open a location in a map app, search a product barcode on the web, copy and share. Scans are saved on this phone, up to 500, with search and delete. Codes it reads and creates: QR code, Aztec, Data Matrix, PDF417, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar and ITF. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Includes a banner ad. English, Korean, light and dark themes are supported.

Product barcode lookup

Tap Look up product on a product barcode result to find its name, brand and size in free public databases. The barcode number is sent only when you choose lookup. Use web search for products that are not found. Information may be missing or out of date, so check the product label.

Data sources: Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts contributors. Open Database License (ODbL 1.0).

Privacy

The camera view, photos and shared images are used only to read codes on this phone; they are not stored or sent anywhere. Scan history is kept only in the app's private storage on this phone and is excluded from automatic Android backup and device transfer. You can turn off saving history in Settings, and history is deleted from the history screen or when you uninstall the app. Codes you create are made on this phone; creating a code does not use the network. Created codes for which you tap Share or Save to gallery are added to the same history. If you choose Save to gallery, the code image is saved to a photo album (DotoriQR). Apps with access to your photos can see it, and it stays after you uninstall the app, so delete it in your gallery. When you tap Look up product on a product barcode result, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts as needed. Those services also receive your IP address, app version and app language. Images, other QR content and scan history are not sent. Lookup never starts automatically. Product results are processed only in app memory, not in files or scan history. Up to 32 recent results are reused for 10 minutes. The providers process requests under their own privacy policy. The optional accessibility service is used only when you tap Share current Wi-Fi. After reading and accepting the explanation in the app, you must enable it yourself in Android settings. It temporarily reads network names, connection status, buttons, and other Wi-Fi settings screen content on the device, then taps the connected network and QR sharing button and stops. Screen information is not saved or sent anywhere, and content on other apps' screens is not read. Device authentication, saving the QR image, and sending it are not automated. You can turn the service off in Accessibility settings at any time. Access is optional: you can still create a QR code by entering the Wi-Fi name and password. There is no developer account, developer server or cloud sync. The only runtime permission requested is the camera; location, contacts, calendar, broad storage and microphone permissions are not requested. Saving a contact, event or Wi-Fi network, calling, texting, emailing, opening a map, searching the web and sharing hand the content to a system app or an app you choose, and that app's own policy applies afterwards. Google AdMob/UMP and Play updates may use the network. Under available privacy choices, the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Settings offers advertising privacy options. Contact droidactor@gmail.com for privacy questions.
