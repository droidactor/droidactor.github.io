Dotori Receipt Scanner

com.droidactor.receipt

Scan one receipt or choose a photo, then check the suggested details before confirming your record. Korean and English text is read on your device, with currencies kept separate.

Coming soonPrivacy

What it does

Capture or choose a photo

Use the document scanner or select one image. Review the merchant, transaction date, amount and currency against the receipt image.

You confirm the record

Recognition can make mistakes. Correct unclear fields yourself and keep unfinished records as drafts. The v1 release uses OCR and extraction rules, without generative AI.

Separate currencies

Review this month’s spending, refunds and net amounts by currency. Drafts and unclassified transactions stay out of totals. There is no exchange-rate conversion.

Share confirmed records

Choose when to share confirmed receipt summaries as CSV. Export includes summary fields and your memo, not individual purchased items. CSV is not a backup or restore format.

Choose image retention

Images are kept in the app by default. Turning retention off applies to future receipts; previously retained images remain until you delete them with their records.

Local recognition

No account or developer-operated receipt server. Scanner setup may need a Play services download, and cloud photo providers may need a connection. SDK diagnostics and ads are separate from receipt recognition.

Known limits

Check every result before confirming it. V1 does not extract individual items, classify purchases into categories, calculate taxes, search records, show totals for earlier months, flag duplicates, or provide cloud sync or restore. First-use offline capture is not guaranteed.

Privacy

Receipt images and recognized text are processed on the device and are not uploaded for recognition or passed to advertising requests. Receipt storage is excluded from cloud backup. Deleting a record removes its retained image, but not your gallery original or CSV copies already shared. Structured card fields keep only the last four digits; images may still contain other printed information. ML Kit diagnostics and AdMob/UMP have separate data practices.
