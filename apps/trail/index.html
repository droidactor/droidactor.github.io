Dotori Korea Trails

com.droidactor.trail

Walk Korea's long-distance walking trails over multiple days without an account, sign-in or subscription. Follow 230 bundled official GPX courses across Korea Dullegil and 6 National Forest Trails, track your multi-day section union progress, see verified nearby restrooms and visitor centers, and keep Today steps separate from Course steps.

A multi-day walking companion for Korea's official trails

Designed to be used alongside official stamp apps while you walk: bundled GPX alignments, multi-day union progress, off-route alerts, verified nearby facilities, screen-off recording and separated step tracking, all stored locally on your phone. Not yet published on Google Play.

  • 10 official trail groups (230 courses): Korea Dullegil OpenAPI courses — Haeparang (44), Namparang (46), Seohaerang (53), DMZ Peace Trail (31) — plus 6 National Forest Trails — Jirisan Dullegil (21), Daegwallyeong (16), Uljin Geumgang Pine (7), Naepo Cultural (5), DMZ Punchbowl Dullegil (4, listed separately from DMZ Peace Trail) and Baekdudaegan Trail (3).
  • Jeju Olle & personal GPX: Jeju Olle routes are not bundled to respect foundation rights. Import your own GPX files through Android's file picker or launch the official Olle Pass app directly.
  • Multi-day section union & out-and-back integrity: walking a course across multiple days combines walked buckets without double-counting repeated segments. On out-and-back courses, each GPS observation credits at most one leg, and pauses or GPS gaps are never bridged. Mid-course starts and reverse walking are supported identically.
  • Course version preservation: each course records its official reference date and geometry hash. When a bundled course alignment is updated, previous judgements remain preserved as a version snapshot rather than being silently overwritten or migrated.
  • Off-route alerts & verified facilities: triggers only when you stay at least 40 m (or twice GPS accuracy) off-route for 10 seconds, with a 60-second repeat cooldown. Only coordinate-verified restrooms and tourist info centers appear as on-route upcoming stops; ambiguous or off-route spots are shown as straight-line references, and trails/courses without official restroom coordinates explicitly display "Toilet info not included."
  • Today steps vs Course steps: displays all-day steps and active walk session steps separately and never sums them. Denying physical activity permission still allows full GPS distance recording.
  • Screen-off foreground recording: keeps recording distance and steps with a persistent notification when the screen turns off, without requesting background location permission. Includes a full-screen walking map mode with the banner hidden.
  • Current Location · 119: shows your WGS84 coordinates, Korea National Point Number, accuracy and timestamp with copy and editable 119 SMS draft actions.

Use it alongside official apps

Official trail completion stamps and certifications are issued only by Korea Tourism Organization's Durunubi app and the Jeju Olle Foundation's Olle Pass app. Dotori Korea Trails is an independent personal walking companion built on Korean public data. It is not affiliated with and does not represent KTO, Korea Forest Service, Jeju Olle Foundation or any government agency. Official app buttons only perform a normal app launch, and personal progress is completely separate from official certification.

Every feature free

All features are free with no account, subscription or in-app purchase. An anchored banner ad is shown at the top of the screen (hidden in full-screen walking map mode), and advertising privacy choices are offered where required.

Before you walk and known limitations

  • Korea Dullegil includes the 174 courses officially opened via Public Data Portal OpenAPI (15101974). Seoul Trail 2.0 is planned for a later update once open geometry data is verified.
  • DMZ Peace Trail, DMZ Punchbowl Dullegil and Uljin Geumgang Pine Forest Trail include reservation-only or ID-required sections; always confirm official entry rules before visiting.
  • This is not turn-by-turn navigation. Trail alignments and facility data come from public datasets with fixed reference dates; always follow on-site signs, closures and safety advisories.
  • Course geometry is bundled offline, but Google Maps base tiles require a network connection.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later.

Data sources

  • Korea Tourism Organization, Durunubi Trail Information OpenAPI (Public Data Portal 15101974): Korea Dullegil 4 routes, 174 official GPX courses.
  • Korea Forest Service / Korea Forest Welfare Institute, National Forest Trails GPX (Public Data Portal 15108080, reference date 2024-12-31): 6 National Forest Trails, 56 courses.
  • Korea Forest Service, Forest Trail Facilities (Public Data Portal 15125108) & MOIS National Cycling Paths DB (3038533): verified public restroom coordinates.
  • National Tourist Information Center Standard Data (Public Data Portal 15013112): tourist information centers.

All bundled datasets are published with unrestricted usage permissions (이용허락범위 제한 없음).

Privacy

Walking tracks, memos, favorites, saved GPX files, course completion snapshots, step history and settings stay in app-private storage on your device and are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. There is no account and no developer server for user records. Precise location is used only while recording a walk or viewing Current Location · 119; background location is not requested. Physical activity permission is optional and used only to count Today steps and Course steps separately. Google Maps, Google AdMob/UMP, Play In-App Updates and signed static operational notices from droidactor.github.io use the network.
