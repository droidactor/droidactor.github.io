Dotori Bluetooth Macro Pad

com.droidactor.bt_macropad

Custom Bluetooth shortcuts and key sequences for your PC.

Turn your phone into a Bluetooth HID macro pad. Send shortcuts to the focused PC window without a PC driver or companion app.

Coming soonPrivacy

Shortcuts within reach

  • 3 ready-to-use sets: Edit, Window & screen, and Browser.
  • Choose from 36 recommended actions or build a shortcut with modifier keys.
  • Combine up to 4 key steps, name and color each button, and choose a 2×3, 3×3, 3×4 or 4×4 grid.
  • Use Edit mode to rename, reorder, duplicate or add sets and swap buttons.

Screens

Before pressing a button

Pair the phone and PC over Bluetooth, connect from the app and focus the PC program to control. You can customize sets while disconnected. Tap Edit before changing a button; normal taps send keys.

Requirements

Requires Android 9.0+ and Bluetooth HID Device support. Default shortcuts target Windows and common PC browsers; key behavior depends on the focused program and operating system. Phone power-saving settings may end a background connection.

Privacy

Macro content and host records stay on the device. Key sequences go directly to the selected PC over Bluetooth. Includes an AdMob banner and UMP privacy choices.
