Dotori Flashlight Camera

com.droidactor.light

Keep the flashlight on while you take photos. LED brightness on supported devices, an auto-off timer with notification controls and a quick settings tile.

Take photos with the light on

Most camera apps turn the flashlight off when they open. Tap Take photos with the light on to open the app's own camera screen with the flashlight still on, frame the shot, tap to focus and pinch to zoom. Photos are saved to Pictures/DotoriLight on this phone, and you can open and share the latest one. The camera screen has no ads. When you close it, the flashlight comes back on if it was on and the timer has not ended; the light may go dark for a moment while switching.

LED brightness

On Android 13 or later, if the device supports brightness steps, a slider changes the LED brightness. Other devices turn on at one fixed brightness and the app says so. Brightness on the camera screen needs a supported device with Android 15 or later.

Auto-off timer and notification

Choose none, 1, 3, 5, 10 or 30 minutes, or a custom time from 1 to 60 minutes. With the screen off, the notification shows the time left with Turn off and +1 min buttons. Cancel the timer in the app to keep the light on.

Quick settings tile

Add the Dotori Flashlight Camera tile to quick settings to turn the light on or off with the app's brightness and timer without opening the app. Requires Android 8.0 or later and a phone with a camera flash. Includes a banner ad on every screen except the camera screen. English, Korean, light and dark themes are supported.

Privacy

The flashlight turns on without camera permission. Camera permission is requested only when you open the photo screen; photos are saved only to the phone's photo folder (Pictures/DotoriLight) and are not sent anywhere by the app. On Android 9 and earlier, storage permission is requested when a photo is saved. Sharing a photo hands it to the app you choose in the system share sheet, and that app's own policy applies afterwards. Notification permission is used only to show that the flashlight is on and the time left on the timer. A foreground service keeps the light on while the screen is off, and a wake lock is held only while an auto-off timer runs. Brightness, timer, startup and shutter sound settings are kept only in the app's private storage on this phone and are excluded from automatic Android backup and device transfer. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes these settings; photos in Pictures/DotoriLight stay in your gallery until you delete them. There is no developer account, server or cloud sync. Location, microphone and contacts permissions are not requested. Photos and flashlight settings are not sent to the developer or the ad SDK. Google AdMob/UMP and Play updates may use the network. Under available privacy choices, the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Settings offers advertising privacy options.
