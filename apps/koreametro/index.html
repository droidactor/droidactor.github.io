Dotori Korea Metro

com.droidactor.koreametro

Choose a subway region in Settings and keep using one app on your next trip.

Choose your region

Switch between the Seoul metropolitan area, Busan, Daegu and Gwangju. The app starts in the Seoul metropolitan area the first time, then remembers your last choice. Your recent stations, favorites and recent routes are kept separately for each region. Language, theme and text size apply across the app.

This is a separate app. The existing Dotori Seoul, Busan, Daegu and Gwangju Metro apps remain available independently; their saved data is not automatically transferred.

Maps and routes on your device

All four regions have bundled maps and station data. Zoom the vector map, search station names and view route guidance without downloading another region. Route planning uses the data available for the selected region.

Timetables, travel times, fares and station facilities have different coverage by region and line. Some official fare or facility lookups require an internet connection. The app does not promise live arrivals or identical information for every region. Daejeon is not included.

Seoul map geometry is based on the Seoul Open Data Plaza OA-22535 source with attribution. The other three regions use original vector layouts based on station data and confirmed topology. Data sources and coverage are available in the app. This is an independent app, not an official transit-operator service.

Optional nearby-station lookup

With your permission, the app uses a foreground location fix to find a nearby station in the region you selected. It does not switch regions automatically or track your journey in the background. Coordinates are processed on your device and are not saved or added to advertising requests. Without a usable location in that region, the map starts at its default station.

Privacy

Region choices, station favorites and recent routes are stored locally. No account or developer-operated journey-history server is used. A Google AdMob banner may process advertising data under the available privacy choices.
