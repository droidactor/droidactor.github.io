Dotori Text Scanner

com.droidactor.textscanner

A free image to text OCR app. Take a photo, or open a gallery photo or screenshot, and the text is recognized on your device so you can tap just the text blocks you need and copy them in the order you picked.

Screens

Tap the text blocks you need and copy them in order

Copy text from menus, notices, posters and book paragraphs instead of typing it again.

  • Tap text blocks to pick and copy: when text recognition finishes, each text block is outlined on the photo. Tap the blocks you need; they are numbered in the order you tap, and Copy copies them in that order. Tap a picked block again to unpick it, use Pick all or Clear picks, or open the Block list checklist when a block is too small to tap. Collect sentences from different places in the order you want without dragging selection handles.
  • Join lines, edit, copy and share: Join lines puts each block on one line (a word split by a hyphen at a line end is joined back); turn it off to keep the lines as recognized. Edit opens the picked text, or all text when nothing is picked, so you can fix it before copying or sharing it to another app.
  • On-device text recognition and auto rotation: Korean and Latin-script languages such as English are recognized on your device with bundled ML Kit, and your photos are not uploaded to a developer server. Tilted or sideways photos are retried at other angles automatically, and Rotate turns the photo 90 degrees and reads it again. Pinch to zoom the photo up to 8x.
  • Camera, gallery and share: photos you take stay in the app's memory and are never saved to your gallery or a file. Tap Gallery to pick a photo, or share an image to the app from a messenger, your gallery or a screenshot. Turn on the light in dark places, tap the preview to focus, and pinch to zoom before you take the photo.

Every feature free

Every feature is free with no account, subscription, in-app purchase or scan limit. A single banner ad appears at the top of every screen and there are no full-screen ads. Advertising privacy options can be reopened in Settings.

Before you start and known limitations

  • On-device recognition may contain errors, so check important text against the original photo.
  • Recognizes Korean and Latin-script languages such as English. It does not read handwriting, translate text, extract text from PDF files or videos, or copy text on top of other apps' screens.
  • Photos and recognized text are kept in memory only while you work on them. Closing the app or retaking a photo clears them, so copy or share what you need first.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later. A camera is optional: gallery and shared images can be read on devices without one.

Privacy

Camera access is used only for taking photos. Photos you take stay in memory and are never saved to your gallery or a file; a gallery photo or shared image is read only when you pick or share it, and no storage permission is requested. Text recognition runs on your device, and photos and recognized text are not sent to a developer server or passed to ad requests, and recognized text is not logged. Settings and operational notice state stay in app-private storage excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Google AdMob/UMP, ML Kit diagnostics, Play updates and signed static operational notices from droidactor.github.io may use the network.
