Tap anywhere to count

The whole screen is the button. The big number and the hints are part of the tap area, so you can keep your eyes on what you count. One finger adds 1, two fingers add 5 and four fingers add 10. The count is decided when all fingers lift, so fingers may land a little apart, and a long press still counts once.

Set your own amounts

In Settings, set the amount for one to five fingers (+1 to +999). Two to five fingers can also be left unassigned. An unassigned finger count is not counted and gives one long vibration. The + buttons at the bottom follow the amounts you set.

Count without looking

Each count vibrates, with a different pattern for each number of fingers. The screen stays on while counting by default, and you can turn on a beep or counting with the volume keys in Settings. If any finger slides, or the system takes over the touch such as with the back gesture, the tap is not counted. With TalkBack, multi-finger taps go to TalkBack, so double-tap the counting area to add the one-finger amount and use the buttons for other amounts.

Undo and multiple counters

Undo reverts the last change as a whole, including a reset to 0. Create named counters and switch between them, and copy or share the names and counts of all counters as text. Counts are saved on this phone after every change. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Includes a banner ad. English, Korean, light and dark themes are supported.

Privacy

Counts, counter names, undo history, the amount per finger count and feedback settings are kept only in the app's private storage on this phone and are excluded from automatic Android backup and device transfer. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. There is no developer account, server or cloud sync. The app itself declares only VIBRATE, for count feedback; the ad SDK adds the network and advertising permissions listed in section 4. Location, camera, microphone, contacts and storage permissions are not requested. Sharing or copying results hands the text to the system share sheet or clipboard when you tap it, and the app you choose applies its own policy afterwards. Counts and counter names are not sent to the developer or the ad SDK. Google AdMob/UMP and Play updates may use the network. Under available privacy choices, the ad SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Settings offers advertising privacy options. Contact droidactor@gmail.com for privacy questions.
