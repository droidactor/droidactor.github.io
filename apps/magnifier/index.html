Dotori Magnifier

com.droidactor.magnifier

A large-button magnifier for reading small print on medicine bags, ingredient labels, manuals and appliance model plates. Opens directly to the live camera with rear LED light, high-contrast color filters, freeze zoom, on-device text recognition and offline read-aloud.

Screens

Large-button magnifier that reads small print aloud

Built for reading tiny print without full-screen or video ads getting in the way when you open the app. Not yet published on Google Play.

  • Live magnification: quick zoom presets matched to your camera's zoom range, a zoom slider with minus and plus buttons, two-finger pinch zoom, tap-to-focus and volume key zoom.
  • Rear LED light and exposure: keep the flashlight on while reading (LED brightness steps appear on supported Android 15 or later devices) and adjust camera exposure to tame glare on glossy labels.
  • 4 color filters: switch between Original, High contrast, Invert and Yellow/Black in live view or on a frozen frame.
  • Freeze screen with optional save: pause a shaking view, pinch up to 8x and pan around comfortably. The LED turns off automatically while frozen (configurable) and restores when you return to Live. Frames stay in memory only unless you tap Save (Pictures/DotoriMagnifier) or Share (temporary cache file).
  • On-device text recognition and large-text reader: recognizes text from the unfiltered frozen frame on your device using bundled ML Kit. Tap a detected text box, choose from the Areas list or press Read all to read in 20–44sp high-contrast palettes and copy or share the text.
  • Offline voice read-aloud: reads recognized text using installed offline device TTS voices at slow, normal or fast speed, with a shortcut to device voice installation if offline voice data is missing.

Every feature free

All features are free with no account, subscription or in-app purchase. The magnifier, freeze and large-text reader screens have no ads; a single banner ad appears only on Settings and Help, and advertising privacy options can be reopened in Settings.

Before you start and known limitations

  • Maximum zoom and LED brightness control depend on your phone's camera hardware; LED brightness steps require Android 15 or later on supported devices.
  • Zooming a frozen frame up to 8x enlarges the display for inspection and does not increase saved image resolution.
  • On-device text recognition may contain errors. Always verify critical medication dosages, expiry dates and labels against the original image.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later and a device with a camera. Does not provide live translation, video recording, front mirror mode or a built-in gallery.

Privacy

Camera access is used only for magnifying, freezing and on-device text recognition. Frozen images stay in memory only unless you tap Save (Pictures/DotoriMagnifier; Android 9 and earlier requests storage access only when saving) or Share (temporary cache file). Text recognition and offline voice playback run on your device without sending images or text to a developer server or ad requests, and recognized text is not logged. Settings and operational notice state stay in app-private storage excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Google AdMob/UMP, ML Kit diagnostics, Play updates and signed static operational notices from droidactor.github.io may use the network.
