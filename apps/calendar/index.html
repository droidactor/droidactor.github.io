Dotori Lunar Calendar

com.droidactor.DrCalendar

Korean lunar calendar with the 24 solar terms, Sambok, Son-free days and Ganji. Register a lunar birthday or ancestral rite once and it lands on the right solar date every year through 2100.

Lunar birthdays on the right date every year

A lunar birthday or memorial day falls on a different solar date every year. Register it once as a lunar yearly event and the app works out its solar date for every year through 2100, then writes those dates into the device calendar you pick, with your reminder.

  • For example, lunar 8/20 falls on 30 September 2026, 20 September 2027 and 8 October 2028 in the calendar data of the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI).
  • Tap a day and then “+ Add”, or long-press a day. Pick the start date as a lunar date, check “All day” and “Lunar yearly”, and the line below shows the repeat, such as “Every year on lunar 8/20”.
  • Ancestral rites held on a lunar date are added the same way.

Screens

Lunar and solar dates on one calendar

  • Month view: lunar dates (every 5 days, daily or hidden), Korean public holidays, the 24 solar terms with their meanings, Sambok (Chobok, Jungbok and Malbok) and traditional days such as Jeongwol Daeboreum, Dano, Chilseok and Baekjung.
  • Son-free days, the lunar days ending in 9 or 0, carry a small red badge with the Korean letter 손.
  • Ganji (10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches) in Hangul, Hanja or both.
  • Week view lays out one week in seven rows, with each day's lunar date, Ganji, holidays and events.
  • Day info shows the Dangun era year, the solar and lunar dates (including leap months) and the year, month and day Ganji.
  • Swipe left or right to change months and up or down to change years, jump to any solar or lunar date, and use the landscape layout and light or dark themes.
  • Lunar dates, solar terms and Ganji are calculated on the phone without internet or calendar access.

Lunar/solar converter

Convert between solar and lunar dates in either direction. The result shows both dates, the Ganji of the day and the days left from today, or the days passed for a past date, and “Go to date” moves the calendar there. Check the leap month box for a date in a leap month; if that year has no such leap month, the app says so instead of converting. The converter covers lunar years 1799 to 2100.

For Korean age, zodiac animals or business days, Dotori Date & Lunar Calculator fits better.

Your device calendar events

  • With calendar permission, events from the calendars synced to your phone, such as Google, Outlook, Exchange or CalDAV, appear alongside the lunar dates. The app does not sign in to those accounts; it reads the calendars your phone already syncs.
  • Choose which calendars to show, list events by calendar and select several events to delete at once.
  • If a new event overlaps another one, the app asks before adding it.
  • “Add to calendar” from another app, such as mail, opens the add screen with the title and time filled in.
  • If there is no calendar to save to, the app can create one that stays only on this phone.

Free with banner ads

The app is free and shows banner ads. No account is needed. It started in 2013 as Ondroid Calendar and was rebuilt and renamed in 2026; settings and lunar anniversaries saved by the earlier version carry over after the update.

Before you start and known limitations

  • A lunar month has 29 or 30 days. An anniversary on the 30th gets no event in a year when that month ends on the 29th.
  • An anniversary saved in a leap month appears only in years that have that leap month.
  • Reminders come from your phone's calendar app, because the events live in the device calendar. If its notifications are off, you see the events but get no reminder.
  • A calendar that stays only on this phone is not synced or backed up. On a new phone, add those anniversaries again.
  • Dates follow Korean lunar calendar data. A Chinese lunar table can differ by a day: KASI lists the first day of the 2027 lunar year as February 7, the Hong Kong Observatory as February 6.
  • KASI notes that its calendar data two years ahead is not yet the official release.
  • Holiday and solar term names and the 손 badge stay in Korean when the app is in English.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later.

Privacy

Solar and lunar dates are calculated on the phone. Calendar access (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR) is used to show the events you already have and to save the lunar anniversaries you add into the device calendar you pick. Device calendar events and the dates you enter are processed on the phone and are not sent to a developer server or the ad SDK. If the calendar you pick syncs with an account such as Google, entries saved there follow that provider's sync. There is no account, sign-in or location permission. Anniversaries saved into a device calendar stay there after you uninstall the app; delete them in your calendar app. Banner ads are served by Google.
