Dotori Learn Korean Numbers

com.droidactor.hannum

Three minutes is 삼 분, three people in polite speech is 세 분, and three animals is 세 마리. Choose what you mean and learn both the reading and its rule.

Features

Compare by meaning

Choose a number and counter to see readings, reasons and accepted alternatives. Clock time combines hours and minutes.

Seven lessons and review

Learn both number systems, ten-thousand grouping, counter forms and everyday expressions. Practise context quizzes, revisit missed rules and save expressions.

Six languages and example audio

Choose English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese or Spanish explanations. Example audio is bundled; other readings use your device Korean TTS voice when available.

Screens

Requirements

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. Learning material and example audio are bundled. Advertising, privacy options and your device TTS engine may need a connection. Free with banner advertising.

Native Korean numbers cover 1–99; Sino-Korean numbers are below 10 quadrillion. Zero-valued clock components are handled separately. Pronunciation aids are available for selected examples.

Privacy

Learning records and saved expressions stay on your device, with automatic backup disabled. Google handles banner advertising and consent services. Read the app-specific policy for details.
