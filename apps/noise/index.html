Dotori Sound Meter & Spectrum

com.droidactor.noise

A free sound meter, decibel meter and audio spectrum analyzer for everyday ambient noise. View real-time dBA/dBC/dBZ levels, a 60-second trend chart, Low/Mid/High bands and a 28-band spectrum, calibrate in 0.1 dB steps and export interval logs as CSV or PNG summary cards.

Real-time decibel meter and frequency spectrum

Designed to let you inspect ambient noise levels and frequency balance without subscriptions, trials or full-screen ads blocking the meter. Not yet published on Google Play.

  • IEC 61672-1 A, C and Z frequency weighting (dBA, dBC, dBZ) and Fast (125 ms) or Slow (1 s) response.
  • Live dB readout, Min / energy-averaged Leq / Max statistics, 60-second history chart and microphone clipping/overload indicator.
  • Unweighted Low (20–250 Hz), Mid (250 Hz–2 kHz) and High (2–16 kHz) band summaries plus an expandable 28-band spectrum with peak frequency (Hz) for relative comparison.
  • Device calibration offset in ±0.1 dB and ±1.0 dB steps (−30.0 to +30.0 dB) with clear calibration state chips (Uncalibrated, Device reference or Calibrated).
  • Free interval recording up to 4 hours per session (1-second numeric summaries only, no raw audio files saved) with UTF-8 CSV and 1080×1440 PNG report card export via save or share.

Every feature free

Every feature—including spectrum view, interval recording and CSV/PNG export—is free with no account, subscription or in-app purchase. A single banner ad is shown at the top of the screen (no interstitial or rewarded ads), with ad app-mute signaling requested and advertising privacy options available in Settings.

Before you start and known limitations

  • Reference tool only: smartphone microphones vary by model and are not laboratory instruments. This app does not replace a certified sound level meter and is not intended for legal noise-dispute evidence, workplace safety compliance or hearing-protection decisions.
  • A single dB offset cannot correct microphone frequency response curves, especially in the low-frequency band; use 3-band and 28-band levels for relative comparison and trend checks.
  • Foreground only: measuring and interval recording run only while the Measure screen stays visible. Leaving the app or turning off the screen pauses recording and closes the microphone.
  • Requires Android 8.0 or later. English and Korean, dark and light themes.

Privacy

The microphone permission is used only while the Measure screen is visible on screen to calculate live sound levels and frequency spectrum. Audio is analyzed in memory on this phone and discarded immediately; raw audio files are never recorded, saved or sent. Interval records (1-second numeric summaries), calibration offset, settings and notice cache stay only in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Exporting CSV or PNG happens only when you pick a destination or app. There is no developer account, server or cloud sync. Google AdMob/UMP, Play updates and signed static operational notices from droidactor.github.io may use the network.
