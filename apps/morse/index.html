Dotori Morse

com.droidactor.morse

Read International, Korean and Japanese Morse from sound, taps or text.

Privacy policy

What it does

  • Microphone, PCM WAV, screen taps and dot-and-dash text
  • International, Korean and Japanese Wabun, selected in Settings
  • Original signals, Korean jamo and alternative readings
  • Separate edited text, 30 local records, copy and tone playback
  • English and Korean UI, light and dark themes

App demo

Watch the English demo

Requirements and limits

Android 8.0+. Foreground microphone sessions last up to 120 seconds. WAV input is limited to 120 seconds and 48 MB.

Korean syllable boundaries and small Japanese kana can be ambiguous. Speech, arbitrary impact sounds, MP3 and video decoding are not supported.

An independent utility, not a government app or emergency service.

Privacy

Input, decoding and saved history stay on your device. Raw microphone recordings are not saved or uploaded. The ad SDK processes separate advertising data.
