Dotori Seoul Metro

com.droidactor.seoulmetro

Find a station, follow a line and compare its name with the Korean on the signs around you. A subway map of Seoul and the surrounding metropolitan area for visitors to Korea, with the map data stored on your phone.

Coming soonPrivacy

Features

A sharp vector map

Zoom into the metropolitan subway map without blurry image pixels. Drag to move, pinch or double-tap to zoom, or use a mouse wheel. Select one or more lines to highlight them and dim the rest.

Search in your language

Find stations by Korean name, Korean initial consonants (ㄱㄴ for 강남), English or romanized names, Japanese, or Simplified and Traditional Chinese. Romanized search tolerates some spelling variants and small typos. Tap a result to move to that station on the map.

Station details

Tap a station to see its Korean name beside your language, the English, Japanese and Chinese names that are available, and the lines that stop there. Show these lines highlights just those lines. When a name is missing in your language, the app shows the English name.

A Korean card to show staff

Show in Korean opens a card with the station's Korean name in large type and the sentence “…역에 가고 싶어요” (“I want to go to … Station”), ready to show to station staff or someone nearby.

Language and label choices

From the menu (⋮), choose English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or your device language, and label the map with Korean plus your language, your language only, or Korean only.

Known limits

This version is a map and station reference. It does not plan journeys or show live arrivals, service alerts, timetables or fares. The bundled map may lag behind service changes, so follow signs and operator announcements when you travel.

An independent app, not an official app of any transport operator, and not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the organisations whose data it uses.

Privacy

Search text, selected stations and lines, and label choices are processed on your device; they are not sent to a server of ours or included in advertising requests. There is no account, no location permission and no saved search history, and Android backup is off. Google handles the banner ad and its privacy choices, which you can reopen from the menu (⋮) → Privacy choices. Read the app-specific policy for details.
