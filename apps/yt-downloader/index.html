Dotori YouTube Downloader

com.droidactor.ytdownloader

Downloads videos and playlists to the location you choose. The request goes from your device to the source directly — never through a server of ours, which also means we never see what you download.

Coming soon Privacy

What it does

Videos and playlists

Paste a URL and the app fetches it. A playlist is handled as a list of items rather than one download you have to babysit.

Your folder, not ours

Files are written to the location you pick through Android's own file picker. That is also why the app asks for no storage permission — it only ever touches what you handed it.

Keeps going in the background

A notification shows progress while a download runs, so you can use another app without the transfer being killed.

No server in the middle

There is no download service of ours to queue behind, rate-limit you, or keep a record of what you asked for.

Using it responsibly

Please respect the terms of service of the source site and the copyright of the material you download. Responsibility for how the app is used rests with the user.

Privacy

No download history is sent anywhere — it stays on the device and goes away with the app. We run no download server and never see the URLs you paste.
