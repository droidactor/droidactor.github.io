Dotori Bluetooth Mouse

com.droidactor.bt_mouse

Use the phone in your hand as a trackpad and three-button mouse. The host sees a standard Bluetooth HID mouse, so there is no receiver, driver, companion app or account.

Coming soon Privacy

One surface for every pointer action

Trackpad gestures

Move the pointer, tap for a click and double-tap for a double-click without changing modes.

Buttons you can hold

Dedicated left, middle and right buttons support press-and-hold actions such as dragging.

Scroll both ways

Separate vertical and horizontal rails, optional natural direction and adjustable pointer sensitivity.

Privacy

Pointer movement, buttons and scroll gestures go directly to the paired host over Bluetooth HID. Inputs are not logged or sent to us. The verified host address stays on the phone for reconnection and is masked on screen. No location permission, text input or clipboard access is used.
