Save places and record your routes

4 November 2026

In short. A place you find on a walk may be easier to remember by its location than its name. Dotori GPS Tracker saves your current position and records the route of a journey on Android. Add notes to places, review a finished journey and export it as GPX for compatible tools. Your location records stay on your phone without an account.

Save a place with a useful note

Check the accuracy and time of the latest location fix before saving a place. Add a note that will help you recognize it later. “Bench beside the fountain” describes a park stop better than an unnamed pin. Notes and journey titles are also searchable when your records grow.

Open a saved point in Google Maps, KakaoMap, TMAP or another compatible map app. To tell someone where it is, copy the coordinates and location link or use sharing. Choose whether to include your note when you share. You decide which records to pass to another app.

Record a journey as you move

Use a location save for a single place and journey recording for a route. Choose a time interval, a distance interval, or both together. In the combined mode, a new point needs both the time and movement conditions to be met. Point counts depend on these settings and the location fixes your device receives.

  1. Start recording in the app before you set off.
  2. Save important places along the way to associate them with the journey.
  3. Pause during a break and resume when you continue.
  4. Finish recording and review the journey summary.

A journey you start can continue recording with the screen off. Allow notifications to use pause, resume and finish controls from the recording notification. Recording does not restart automatically after a reboot or force stop. Check that the journey is finished when you are done.

Review the map and the route drawing

The main-screen map shows saved places and routes. The summary shows distance, elapsed time and moving time. It also shows average and maximum speed, elevation gain and point count. Separate elapsed and moving times help distinguish the whole outing from time spent moving. These values are calculated from the location data the device receives.

The journey preview draws a route and its points without a map background. It is a separate view from the main-screen map. Screenshots here use synthetic records at public places rather than a person's movement history. Device and reception differences can produce different results on the same route.

Export GPX and back up your records

Export a journey and its saved points as GPX for compatible apps and tools. For a backup of all records, use JSON export and restore. Restoring a JSON backup replaces existing records after confirmation. Back up anything you want to keep first. GPX export and a full records backup serve different purposes.

Coordinates, routes and notes are kept in app-private storage on the device. Map display, external maps, sharing and exports have their own service data practices. A banner may show ads, whose data is handled separately. GPS coordinates, route history and notes are not included in advertising requests.

The app requires Android 8.0 or later and location permission. Map display may need a network connection. Accuracy depends on the device, surroundings and permission settings. Lost reception can leave gaps. The app does not guarantee emergency assistance or professional surveying accuracy.
