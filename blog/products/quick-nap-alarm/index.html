A 15-minute nap alarm, one tap away

5 November 2026

In short. A short rest after lunch should not need another alarm's hour and minute. Open Quick nap in Dotori Alarm Clock. Tap +15 minutes to schedule an alarm for 15 minutes from now. You do not need to calculate a clock time or open a new alarm editor. Keep short rest alarms and your usual morning alarms in the same Android app.

Tap the duration you want

The Quick nap tab on the main screen offers 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 minutes. Tapping a duration immediately schedules a one-time alarm. Time starts when you tap, rather than when you fall asleep. If you choose 15 minutes, the first ring is scheduled for 15 minutes after that tap.

The list shows the nap label, its scheduled time and the time remaining. Check it alongside your workday and weekend alarms, and edit or delete it when needed. Adding a nap does not require changing the time of an existing repeating alarm.

  1. Open Quick nap on the main screen.
  2. Tap a duration, such as +15 minutes.
  3. Check the scheduled time and time remaining in the list.

Keep your usual alarm settings

A quick nap uses your default alarm sound, volume, vibration and snooze policy. Choose the usual repeat limit, waiting interval and ring duration in Settings first. These defaults let you start another nap without choosing the same options every time.

For an alarm that needs a separate policy, turn off Follow app defaults in its editor. The snooze limit counts additional rings after the first. Choose zero to disable snooze, select 1–10 repeats, or use Unlimited within the current occurrence. Wait intervals range from 1 to 60 minutes. An optional step-down shortens later waits by one or two minutes. The minimum is one minute.

Preview the upcoming rings

Before saving, the schedule preview shows the first ring and upcoming snoozes, up to six steps. It includes both the time spent ringing and the waiting interval. Check expected times without adding the settings together yourself.

The preview assumes that each timed ring runs without a response. Snoozing manually or pausing for an overlapping alarm can change the actual times. When another alarm starts, the earlier alarm waits in a queue. Its remaining ring time and snooze progress are preserved.

Use the timer and stopwatch too

For a countdown, open Quick timer. Start a preset or enter hours, minutes and seconds. Check the time remaining for each running timer. Each timer ends automatically with one short beep. Your nap alarm's snooze policy does not apply to timers.

The stopwatch supports start, pause, resume and reset. Record up to 100 laps with split and cumulative times. Timing continues when you leave and reopen the app. For a regular clock-time alarm, use the wheel or type the digits. Check the next ring before saving.

No ads on ringing and dismissal screens

Ringing, snooze and dismissal screens have no ads. No interstitial appears after dismissal. The main list displays a banner. Alarm settings and recent completion results stay on your device without an account. See the privacy policy for advertising SDK data handling.

Check permissions and alarm volume in System check before using the app. Try the 10-second test ring. Alarms cannot ring while the phone is powered off or the app is force-stopped. Manufacturer battery management can also affect delivery. The app does not detect when you fall asleep or measure sleep benefits.
