Korea Dullegil & Forest Trails GPX App

27 November 2026

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.droidactor.trail

In short. Walking coastal trails over several weekends gets hard when apps lock maps behind a fee. Dotori Korea Trails is a free Android hiking app with no sign-up, login or subscription. It bundles 230 official GPX courses across Korea Dullegil and six National Forest Trails. I built this GPX trail tracker to pair with official stamp apps while keeping records local.

Dotori Korea TrailsWalk Korea Dullegil & national forest trails across days

The app centers on four screens: trail list, trail map, course details and live walking. You browse ten official trail groups with total distance and personal progress. Each trail opens a completion map showing finished, in-progress and unwalked courses. Before starting, you inspect verified nearby restrooms and visitor centers on the line. While walking, it records with the screen off, sends off-route alerts and splits step counts.

Browse 230 official courses across Korea Dullegil and National Forest Trails

Every bundled route comes from unrestricted datasets on Korea's Public Data Portal. For Korea Dullegil, the app includes all 174 courses published in the KTO Durunubi OpenAPI. That covers 44 courses on the Haeparang Trail, 46 on Namparang and 53 on Seohaerang. It also includes 31 official courses on the DMZ Peace Trail.

Six National Forest Trails from the Korea Forest Service add another 56 official courses. They include Jirisan Dullegil (21), Daegwallyeong (16) and Uljin Geumgang Pine (7). Naepo Cultural (5), DMZ Punchbowl Dullegil (4) and Baekdudaegan Trail (3) round out the list.

DMZ Peace Trail and DMZ Punchbowl Dullegil sit in separate groups as separate networks. Jeju Olle routes are not bundled out of respect for the Jeju Olle Foundation's rights. Instead, import your own GPX files into saved GPX to follow them on the map. You can also tap a standard launch button to open the official Olle Pass app directly.

Paint a multi-day completion map without double counting

Long Korean walking trails rarely finish in one outing. Many walkers split a single 18 km course across two separate weekends. Open any trail group to see a color-coded completion map of your progress. Completed, in-progress and unwalked courses stand out right away on one overview.

When you do a multi-day walk on one course, the app combines walked segments as a union. Retracing part of a trail never inflates your finished percentage. On out-and-back overlapping sections, each GPS point credits at most one leg. Pauses and signal gaps are never bridged with fake straight lines.

Starting mid-course or walking in reverse counts identically to a standard start. Each course also stores its official reference date and geometry hash. If a future update changes an official route line, past completion records stay intact. The app preserves those earlier judgments as snapshots instead of overwriting them.

Follow courses with off-route alerts and verified restroom guidance

Missing an unmarked turn on a quiet coastal road wastes time and energy. During a walk, the off-route alert triggers after 10 continuous seconds off the line. The distance threshold is at least 40 meters or twice your current GPS accuracy. A 60-second cooldown stops repeated alerts while you walk back toward the trail.

Nearby restrooms and tourist info centers come from three Korean public datasets. Those are Forest Service trail facilities, MOIS bike path toilets and national info centers. Verified spots along the main path appear with on-route distance ahead. Ambiguous spots near forks or across rivers move to a separate straight-line list.

Some trails have zero official restroom coordinates in those public datasets. DMZ Punchbowl Dullegil is one example in version 1.0. For those trails, the screen clearly shows the Toilet info not included notice. You never mistake missing public data for a trail with zero physical restrooms.

Record walks with the screen off and separate Today from Course steps

On a five-hour walk, your phone stays locked in a pocket most of the day. Once you start a walk, a foreground service keeps recording with the screen off. A persistent notification stays visible, and no background location permission is requested. Full-screen walking map mode hides the top banner so the trail map fills the screen.

The built-in step counter keeps Today steps and Course steps strictly separate. It never adds your all-day total into the active session count. Physical activity permission is optional, so denying it still records full GPS tracks. For safety, the Current Location · 119 screen shows coordinates and Korea's National Point Number. You can copy your fix or open an editable 119 SMS draft with one tap.

To log your first course, follow these four steps:

  1. Pick an official trail group and course, or import your own GPX file.
  2. Check the distance, entry notices and verified facility coverage on the course view.
  3. Tap Start Walk, grant location access (and optional activity access) and lock your screen.
  4. End the session to save locally, then export the GPX file whenever you need a backup.

Official stamps and certificates come only from Durunubi and Olle Pass. Dotori Korea Trails is an independent tool with no official agency affiliation. Its official app buttons perform a normal app launch and pass no walk data. Your personal completion progress stays completely separate from official certification.

Korea Dullegil includes the 174 courses published in the Public Data Portal OpenAPI. Seoul Trail 2.0 is reserved for a later update once open geometry is verified. DMZ Peace Trail, DMZ Punchbowl and Uljin Geumgang Pine include restricted zones. Check official reservation and ID rules before traveling to those courses. This is not turn-by-turn navigation, so follow local signs and closures first.
