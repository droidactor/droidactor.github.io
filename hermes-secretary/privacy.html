Hermes Secretary privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-28. Applies only to Hermes Secretary.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Hermes Secretary reads Gmail messages and settings and Google Calendar calendars and events through the read-only scopes gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly. It only connects the developer's own Google accounts.

How the data is used

  • To list the owner's recent mail and calendar events and to prepare the owner's morning briefing and hourly change notices.
  • Briefings are delivered only to the owner's own messenger account. To compose them, the content may be processed by the AI model service the owner has configured.
  • Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. The data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Hermes Secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable by the program alone. Briefing state is kept there to avoid repeating notices. Nothing is stored on a server operated by droidactor.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the local token files also ends access.
